    Your privacy.

    The documents people bring to Northcue can feel personal. We treat them that way. Here is how we look after them, simply.

    1. You upload
    3. Northcue structures it
    5. AI helps phrase it clearly
    7. It is deleted

    Your document is deleted after we read it. We keep only anonymous information, never its contents.

    The thinking is ours

    Northcue is not a chatbot. We built our own system that reads your document, works out what it appears to be, and carefully decides what belongs on each cue card and how gently to word it. That structure, and the safety behind it, is Northcue's own work. An AI provider only helps put it into clear, friendly language at the end.

    What we keep

    Only anonymous information, like the type of document and whether it read clearly. Never linked to you.

    What we never keep

    Your document, its text, or your name, address, and account numbers.

    How your cards are made

    Northcue structures your cue cards and decides what matters. To help phrase them clearly, an AI provider reads what is on your document. That text is only used to make your cards, it is processed securely, it is not used to train AI, and it is not stored.

    Questions

    If you have any questions about your privacy, you will find how to reach us in the full privacy details.

    Northcue is a reading aid. It does not give legal, financial, or official advice. Always check your original document.

    There's AI everywhere. Why is this different?

    Built for the moment an official envelope makes your chest tighten.

    Your document is never kept.

    Your file is deleted as soon as it is read. What stays behind is anonymous, never the words on your letter.

    AI never decides your safety.

    A fixed set of rules decides what your document appears to be and how carefully to treat it. AI is only allowed to soften the wording, never to change the judgement.

    It knows when to say I'm not sure.

    Northcue will not reassure you about a serious letter, and will not alarm you about a routine one. When it cannot tell, it says so honestly.

    Sometimes the safest answer is no.

    If an upload looks like a scam or does not look like a real letter, Northcue declines instead of guessing. Refusing is part of how it keeps you safe.

    Never a chatbot. Never advice. Never invents what is not in your document.
