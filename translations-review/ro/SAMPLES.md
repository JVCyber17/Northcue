# Romanian pattern samples, machine draft pending human review

All 51 slot templates from templates-en.js next to the Romanian drafts in templates-ro.js, each with two filled examples using plausible UK values.
Slot values are inserted verbatim, exactly as the app does it, so names, amounts and dates stay unchanged inside the Romanian sentences. Some label style slots (type_label, topic and similar) are shown here with English values on both sides. In the live app those labels are translated separately before insertion, so read them as placeholders, not as final wording.
No em or en dashes anywhere in this file, commas and full stops instead.
